# Wow Salon — Billing App: Quick Guide

## One-time setup on the reception laptop

1. Open **web.whatsapp.com** in Chrome, tap *Linked devices → Link a device* on the salon phone, scan the QR code. Leave this tab open all day — invoice messages are sent through it.
2. Open the billing app (your salon's web address) in another tab and log in with the email and password the owner gave you. Tick **Remember me**.

## Making a bill (about 30 seconds)

1. Click **New Bill**.
2. Type the customer's **mobile number**. If they have visited before, their name fills in automatically. If not, type their name.
3. Pick the services: use the **search box** or the category chips. Click a service to add it; click again to add one more. You can change the price or quantity on the right.
4. Optional: add a discount (₹ or %), choose the stylist, add a note.
5. Choose how they paid: **Cash / UPI / Card / Other**.
6. Click **Save & Preview** (or press Ctrl+S).

## Sending the invoice on WhatsApp

1. On the invoice page click **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp Web opens in a new tab with the customer's number and a ready-made message containing the invoice link.
2. Press **Enter** to send. Come back to the app — the invoice is marked as *Sent*.
3. The customer taps the link on their phone to see the invoice and can download a PDF.

If the button shows a warning about the "App URL", ask the owner to set it in **Settings**.

## Mistakes

- Bills cannot be edited. Ask the owner to **Void** the wrong invoice (with a reason) and use **Duplicate** to make a corrected one.
- Voided invoices are not counted in the day's earnings.

## Daily expenses

**Expenses → quick-add**: date, category (Products, Rent, Salary, Electricity, Tea/Snacks, Misc), what it was for, amount, how it was paid.

## End of day

**Reports → Daily Statement** shows today's earnings by payment mode, the invoices, the expenses and the **net**. "Cash in hand" = cash earnings − cash expenses. Print it or download the PDF.

## For the owner

- **Services**: click any price to change it; Enter saves. New prices apply to the next bill only — old invoices never change. Turn a service off instead of deleting it.
- **Settings**: salon details and logo (they appear on invoices), the WhatsApp message text, invoice prefix, GST % (leave 0 if not registered), users and stylists.
- **Reports → Monthly** and **Services** show trends and top services; every report has **Export CSV**.
